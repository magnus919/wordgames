# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and
levels of experience. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and unwanted personal contact are not welcome.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project. Do not post credentials,
private personal information or exploit details publicly. Maintainers may edit
or remove abusive content, close discussions or restrict participation to
protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer via
the contact options on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/magnus919).
